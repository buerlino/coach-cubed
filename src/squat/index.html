What this prototype estimates

Motion: a bundled pose detector tracks an uploaded clip locally. Squat measurements use the more visible body side and camera-plane geometry. Front views are excluded from muscle hypotheses.

Anatomy: BodyParts3D bone and muscle surfaces follow each tracked limb independently. Pelvis, sacrum/L5–S1, L3, T12, T6 and C7 reference points articulate the trunk. These points are inferred unless manually corrected; depth and individual vertebral motion are not measured. Static atlas skinning is approximate.

Activation colours: provisional rules combine joint angles, movement phase and a holding component. The result is a relative estimate, not EMG, force, a percentage of maximum activation, or a prediction of failure. Load, personal strength and individual muscle physiology are not calibrated.

Rep review: the app reports observable changes and limited hypotheses. A slow rep or perceived weak muscle alone cannot identify the limiter. Incomplete motion is not automatically labelled failure.

Lifter feedback: recorded per rep; can add six ranking points only to a hypothesis already supported by motion. These points are not probabilities. Feedback never affects the colours.

Demo: synthetic movement for testing the interface. It provides no evidence of accuracy on real lifters. The rules require validation on labelled squat recordings.

Privacy: video inference and feedback stay in your browser; 3D reconstruction sends pose coordinates to the local server on your device. Export saves a local JSON file containing pose landmarks and any feedback, but not the original video. Reloading clears the session.

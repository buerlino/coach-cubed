M1–M3, what changes

  • The steps are the buttons. 1 Start, 2 End, 3 Analyse, in one row at the bottom, where the thumb is. The next one to press is white; a set one turns green and shows its time; 3 turns green-filled once both are set. "Set start", "Set end" and the step list above the strip are gone.
  • Undo works at any point, from the first mark on, in a fixed slot left of the steps. It is greyed out, never hidden, while there is nothing to undo.
  • Nothing moves. Header, strip, text line and buttons have fixed heights; only the video takes what is left. A set mark, an error or the time estimate changes text in place. A test compares every control's position before and after each mark.
  • Fits the phone. No scrolling in portrait from 640 px high (the Fairphone in Brave is about 760). Phone landscape still scrolls, with the ⌄ cue.
  • The fine steps stay (‹ one frame ›), on both sides of the text line, right under the strip they move.
  • The lift is shown, not switched: its icon and name in the header.

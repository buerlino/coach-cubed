# d62: H's wordmark clears the status bar (dirac, sonnet, branch `wp/dirac-safe-area-top`)
**Why:** `c26` (report `a51b9e8`, "Needs a decision 5"; Fairphone 6, Android 16, WebView 153): in the APK, H's "coach"
wordmark (app/src/ui/screens/H.mjs:56, `.wordmark` in `.screen__header`) sits under the status bar's icons. nordmu,
2026-11-04: pad by `env(safe-area-inset-top)` in the shell. Why it happens:
- app/index.html:6 already has `viewport-fit=cover`, and app/capacitor.config.json sets nothing for Capacitor's
  SystemBars plugin, so it runs with its default `insetsHandling: "css"`. In Capacitor 8.5.3 (`SystemBars.java` in
  `@capacitor/android`, under app/node_modules/ once installed: `WEBVIEW_VERSION_WITH_SAFE_AREA_FIX = 140` at line 39,
  the choice at line 194) a WebView of 140 or later with `viewport-fit=cover` is drawn edge to edge and gets the real
  insets in `env(safe-area-inset-*)`; an older one (the S8+'s Chrome 101) is padded natively and its insets are 0.
  The "css" mode also sets `--safe-area-inset-*` on the root (lines 243–270); `env()` is enough here.
- So on WebView 153 the page reaches under the status bar and nothing pads its top: app/src/styles/base.css uses
  `env(safe-area-inset-bottom, 0px)` six times (lines 249, 289, 311, 342, 378, 537) and `safe-area-inset-top` nowhere.
  The top is the shell's: every screen is a child of `#app` (base.css:30), and H, Welcome and the placeholder screens
  draw the wordmark in `.screen__header`. So the fix goes in the shell, for every screen, not only H.
- The full-height screens are `100dvh` high: `.mark` (base.css:221), `.setup, .live` (267), `.replay` (328), `.delayed`
  (363), `.result` (394, 397, 578), `.detail` (448). Padding `#app` alone would make each of them the inset taller than
  the window, and M, A1 and A2 would scroll again (d54's and d58's rule; c26 measured scrollHeight = innerHeight on
  every walk).
- Where the top inset is 0 (a browser tab, which c26's Brave walks used, and the S8+'s natively padded WebView),
  `env()` gives 0 and nothing changes. The PWA's standalone window wasn't measured (c26, "Needs a decision 7").

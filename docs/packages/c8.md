# c8: the store (curie, sonnet, branch `wp/curie-store`)
**Why:** Lifts, their cut videos, tracks and results stay on the phone (rules-core.md, "Nothing leaves the phone");
docs/app-plan.md ("The pieces") keeps them in OPFS, one folder per lift and an index, with `persist()`, and the quota
shown in S1. Contract 3 (a stored lift) is in `app/src/contracts/`. S1's defaults and A3's view options need a small
settings record too.
**Read:** docs/app-plan.md ("The contracts", "The pieces"); docs/ui-design.md ("The screens": H, A3, S1; "Open
questions"); app/README.md; the stored lift's contract, app/src/contracts/stored-lift.mjs (s5: `validateStoredLift`, and
`liftPaths(id)`, the layout `lifts/<id>/cut.mp4`, `track.json`, `result.json` that the store follows).
**Red first:** yes
1. Tests first, `tests/app/store.test.js`, against an in-memory fake of the OPFS calls the store uses: save a lift (its
   video, track and result), list today's and earlier lifts newest first, read one back, update its state, delete it with
   its folder; the index rebuilt from the folders when it is missing or damaged; the settings record read with v1's
   defaults (decisions.md, D2: a 20 s replay, a 60 s buffer, videos kept) and saved; usage and quota; a full disk
   reported as an error the UI can show (D3, "storage full"). They fail on phase-0.
2. `app/src/store/` over `navigator.storage.getDirectory()`: the index written atomically (write, then move), `persist()`
   asked at the first save and its answer kept, `estimate()` for usage and quota. The fake lives in `tests/app/` only.
3. app/README.md: the layout on the phone and what deleting removes.
**Done when:**
- a stored lift passes contract 3's validator; nothing is written outside OPFS;
- `npm test`, `npm --prefix app test` and the Python tests pass.

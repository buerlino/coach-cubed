# s9: the frame source (mediabunny) (schrödinger, sonnet, branch `wp/schroedinger-frame-source`)
**Why:** Marking (M1–M3), the player (A1) and the analysis job all need frames from a video, fast. On the Fairphone,
seeking a `<video>` cost more than the pose model (map.md in `docs/`, "Phone test"), and WebCodecs with a demuxer worked
in the spike. Contract 5 (a frame source) is in `app/src/contracts/`. Imported videos vary: HEVC, rotation, variable
frame rates, 60 fps (docs/app-plan.md, "Risks"). The demuxer is mediabunny, decided (nordmu, 2026-11-01, D6): one
library for imports, s32's cut of the marked range and the recordings of milestone B.
**Read:** spike/pose-bench/lib/decode.mjs; spike/capture-check/README.md (mediabunny: what it reads and writes);
docs/app-plan.md ("The contracts", "Risks"); app/README.md; THIRD_PARTY.md; the frame source's contract in
`app/src/contracts/`.
**Red first:** yes
1. Test clips of our own: ffmpeg's test pattern (`testsrc2`), H.264 in MP4, a few seconds at 30 fps with a keyframe
   every second, and one with a 90° rotation in its metadata; a few kB each, committed in `tests/app/clips/`, the command
   that made them in tests/README.md.
2. Tests first, `tests/app/frame-source.test.js`: the metadata (duration, rate, size, rotation), the frame times, a
   range's frames in order from the keyframe before it, the frame at a time, thumbnails. What can run in Node runs there
   (demuxing, the sample table, the keyframe search); a fake decoder stands in for WebCodecs. They fail on phase-0.
3. `app/src/media/`: the frame source over WebCodecs, with mediabunny as the demuxer (the spike's capture check used
   1.61.6; pin a version and say which), vendored as an ES module with its licence text. MPL-2.0 is compatible with
   GPL-3.0-or-later unless the source carries the "Incompatible With Secondary Licenses" notice: check its files for it,
   and if it's there, stop and report under "Needs a decision". List it in THIRD_PARTY.md's section "In the new app
   (`app/`)". Rotation is applied once, at the source, so every consumer sees upright frames in their own pixel
   coordinates.
4. What should be measured on the Fairphone (a range's decode rate, the time to one frame at a random time) goes in the
   report, for c26.
**Done when:**
- the frame source passes contract 5's validator; a video it can't decode (HEVC where the WebView lacks it) fails with a
  clear error, never a hang;
- mediabunny is vendored pinned with its licence, carries no "Incompatible With Secondary Licenses" notice, and has its
  row in THIRD_PARTY.md's new-app section; nothing else demuxes;
- `npm test`, `npm --prefix app test` and the Python tests pass.

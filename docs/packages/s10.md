# s10: the pose engine (schrödinger, sonnet, branch `wp/schroedinger-pose-engine`)
**Why:** The working pick is MoveNet SinglePose Thunder on TF.js, every frame, WebGL by default (decisions.md, "What
0.1.0 is"); the spike runs it in a worker behind one async `estimate()`. The app needs that engine in `app/src/pose/`,
trimmed to MoveNet, on s6's from-source bundle.
**Read:** spike/pose-bench/pose-worker.mjs; spike/pose-bench/lib/pose-client.mjs; spike/pose-bench/lib/engines.mjs (the
MoveNet path only); docs/app-plan.md ("The pieces"); app/README.md; the pose messages' contract in `app/src/contracts/`.
**Red first:** yes
1. Tests first, `tests/app/pose-engine.test.js`, with a fake worker: init and ready, a result matched to its call's id,
   replies out of order, a worker error rejects that call only, close, each frame transferred and closed once; keypoints
   that aren't COCO-17 `[y, x, conf]` in frame pixels are refused (contract 1). They fail on phase-0.
2. `app/src/pose/`: the worker (MoveNet Thunder through pose-detection, its crop from the last pose, its keypoint
   smoothing off as in the spike, since `src/track/` smooths), the client, the backend (WebGL; WebGPU and WASM come
   later), a warm-up on a blank frame at init so the first real frames don't pay for compiling shaders, and `reset()`
   between clips so no crop carries over. The weights from `app/models/` and the bundle from `app/vendor/tfjs/`, by
   relative URLs. Copy and trim the spike's code; import nothing from `spike/`.
3. A check in headless Brave on the laptop (software WebGL, a few frames only): the engine loads and returns 17
   keypoints for a synthetic frame. The real numbers wait for c26.
**Done when:**
- the messages pass contract 1's validator; nothing under `spike/` is imported;
- `npm test`, `npm --prefix app test` and the Python tests pass.

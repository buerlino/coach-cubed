# c61: one camera at a time, a Start that says when it didn't, and a failed camera that keeps the lift (curie, opus, branch `wp/curie-capture-races`)
**Why:** a read-only review of milestone B (2026-11-03, after d58) found three bugs in the capture and the session, each
confirmed by a run on the fake camera (`tests/app/fake-webcodecs.js`):
1. A double tap on "Camera stopped" (R2, R3b, R1) or "Try again" (R1) opens two cameras. `capture.resume()` and
   `capture.open()` (app/src/media/capture.mjs, about lines 295–341) check only the state, and the state does not
   change while `openCamera()` is awaited, so a second call passes the check too. `adoptCamera()` replaces `stream`
   without stopping the old one, and `startSession()` replaces the encoder session without closing the first: the first
   camera stays live, and two encoders write into the same ring, so a replay or R3b decodes interleaved chunks. After
   `close()` the first camera and one encoder are still open. The callers don't guard either: `R2.mjs` about 271,
   `R3b.mjs` about 152, `R1.mjs` about 168 and 203.
2. `capture.start()` returns quietly when the state changed while the encoder configs were checked
   (`if (state !== 'preview') return;   // closed while asking`), and app/src/session.mjs (about lines 78–80) then marks
   the session active anyway; `record-model.mjs` (about 115) goes to `live`, and `flow.mjs` (about 187) allows it
   because a session counts as running. (a) Back during Start: the app lands on R2 with no capture, the clock frozen at
   0:00, Replay failing, and H offering "Back to the session". (b) The camera lost during Start: `resume()` with no
   ring goes back to `preview`, so R2 shows a live picture that records nothing, with no "Camera stopped" chip.
3. A capture that fails during a session (another app holds the camera on resume: `camera-busy`; or `encoder-failed`
   while recording) is a dead end: it stays `failed` with the ring kept, `resume()` runs only from `interrupted`, and
   R2 (about 270) and R3b (about 148) show the problem panel with no button, though `camera-busy`'s text says "then try
   again" (`problem-model.mjs` about 73–78). Replay (R2 about 274) and Keep (R3b about 160) are disabled although the
   ring's snapshot still works. The `resume()` rejection is not caught in the three onClick handlers. End session is
   the only way on, and it throws the lift away.
   **nordmu, 2026-11-03:** Try again and keep the lift. `resume()` also runs from `failed` while a ring is held; R2 and
   R3b get a Try again button there; Replay and Keep stay enabled while the ring holds video; the rejections are caught
   and shown as the problem panel.

**Red first:** yes
1. Tests first, on the fake camera, each failing on phase-0:
   - two `resume()` calls at once from `interrupted` (and two `open()` calls at once) leave exactly one live track and
     one encoder session; after `close()` no track is live and no encoder open; the ring holds one encoder's chunks
     only (every group starts on a keyframe, timestamps rising);
   - `session.start()` while the capture is closed during the encoder check, and while the camera ends during it:
     the session is not active, the flow does not go to R2 `live`, and the problem shows (or the app stays on R1);
   - a `resume()` that fails with `camera-busy` while a ring is held: the capture is `failed` with the ring, a second
     `resume()` from there reopens the camera and records on into the same ring from a new keyframe; `snapshot()`
     works while `failed`; record-model offers Try again, Replay and Keep in that state; a failed `resume()` from a
     click is caught (no unhandled rejection) and shown;
   - the same for `encoder-failed` while recording.
2. The change: `open()` and `resume()` hand a second caller the call in flight (one promise per operation), or the
   capture enters a transient state (`opening`) that the guards refuse; `adoptCamera()` stops a previous stream it
   replaces and `startSession()` closes a previous session, as a safety net. `start()` throws a `CaptureError` (or
   returns a result the session checks) when the state changed during the encoder check, and `session.start()` marks
   the session active only when its capture is recording. `resume()` runs from `failed` while a ring is held; R2 and
   R3b show Try again in the problem panel there (the same button as R1's, `data-record="retry"`, nothing moves);
   Replay and Keep stay enabled while the ring holds video. Each onClick that calls `resume()` catches the rejection.
   Fix `camera-busy`'s text if it then promises something the screen doesn't offer.
3. The README's capture and R2/R3b sections say what changed, with `c61`; tests/README.md names the new tests.

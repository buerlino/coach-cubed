1. Tests first, failing on phase-0 (plain modules, in Node):
   - the flow has a state `setup` with the screen ID `R1` (module `screens/R1.mjs`). `home -> setup`, `setup -> live`
     and `setup -> home` are legal, and `home -> live` is not (a session starts on R1). `live -> replay`,
     `live -> home`, `live -> queued` and `queued -> live` stay as they are;
   - the session (a plain module, e.g. app/src/session.mjs, on a fake capture): the app has one, made in main.mjs
     like the queue, and it outlives the screens. `start(lift)` starts the capture and keeps the lift and the start time;
     `end()` stops and closes the capture. `active` and `lift` say what runs, and a second `start` while one runs is
     refused. The camera opens only when R1 is first shown, never at boot (no permission prompt before the user asks);
   - R1's view: the lifts are the registry's, in its order, with their labels and `provisional`. The default is S1's
     `defaultLift`, and the first lift while the settings load or when they name a lift the registry lacks (as d53's
     L1). Each lift's framing tip is the registry's `framing` (s49; v1's two tips), with the line "Whole body inside the
     frame" alone for a lift without one. The line under the preview comes from the settings (`1080p · 30 fps · last
     60 s`). Start is enabled only while the capture shows the preview;
   - R2's view: the clock from the capture's `sinceS` (`m:ss`, `h:mm:ss` from an hour on), and the real rate
     (`fps`, rounded, e.g. `29 fps`), never the asked one. The queue line comes from the jobs (`1 analysing · 1 waiting`,
     nothing when the queue is empty). The lift and the buffer: `Lateral raise · last 60 s`. The wake lock: held is
     `Screen stays on`; refused is `Screen may turn off`. Interrupted shows `Camera stopped` and offers resume
     (the capture's `resume()`). The Replay button carries the replay's length from the settings (`20 s`);
   - the capture's errors on R1 go through d23's problem panel. app/src/ui/problem-model.mjs gains the kinds
     `camera-denied`, `no-camera`, `camera-busy`, `no-encoder` and `insecure`, each with what the user can do, in d55's
     short style;
   - H: the gym card opens R1. While a session runs, it reads "Back to the session" with the clock, and goes to `live`;
   - S1: the replay mode, the replay's length, the buffer and the camera are enabled and saved, within the settings'
     `RANGES`. The delay stays disabled until `d29b`, and the quality stays information (its one value).

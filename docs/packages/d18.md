# d18: A2, a cue's detail (dirac, sonnet, branch `wp/dirac-cue-detail`)
**Why:** A2 shows the worst moment with the cue drawn on the video, the cue's plot over time against its zones, its tiles,
the cue and how it's scored (docs/ui-design.md, A2). s4's `drawCue`, `tiles` and `referenceLine` give the content;
s39's `scoringLines` gives the "How it's scored" block (the old panel's transparency lines as plain text: s4's report,
item 5) and its `zones` the plot's zones. The plot is the app's own, mobile-first (the old panel's `mount()` isn't
reused). Screens follow the mockups (docs/ui-design.md, design/mockups/); what they don't settle goes under "Needs a decision", and screenshots stay out of git: describe them.
**Read:** docs/ui-design.md; design/mockups/CueDetail.dc.html; app/README.md ("Contracts", "Scoring");
app/src/contracts/cue-view.mjs (contract 4: the cue view, `smoothing`); app/src/scoring/score.mjs (`scoreLift`, its
`computed`); app/src/flow/flow.mjs; app/src/ui/app.mjs; d16's and d17's modules in `app/src/draw/` and A1's screen
(by their files, as they left them); s4's and s39's API on the `Diagnostics` object and the old panel's `drawChart`
for what a plot shows (the phase, the two sides' band, the reference line), both in `diagnostics.js` in `src/`, by
section.
**Red first:** yes
1. Tests first, `tests/app/cue-detail.test.js`: the plot's geometry as plain functions (time and value scales, the zones
   from s39's `zones(id)`, none when it gives `null`, the phase band, the reference line, the worst moment's marker)
   against a fake 2D context; the tiles and the texts straight from s4's API, and the "How it's scored" lines from
   s39's `scoringLines` with the view's σ. They fail on phase-0.

   (`scoringLines(id, computed, {smoothingMs: view.smoothing.sigmaMs})`, so its "Time smoothing" line is the σ the
   verdicts were scored with: s35), "provisional" where it is. `computed` is d16's: `scoreLift()` on the stored track
   with the view's own `smoothing`.

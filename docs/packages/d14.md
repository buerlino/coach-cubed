# d14: M1–M3, marking a lift (dirac, sonnet, branch `wp/dirac-mark`)
**Why:** nordmu's design: drag the video under a fixed playhead, Set start, Set end, then Analyse; the marks as tight as
possible, since processing time grows with their length (docs/ui-design.md, "nordmu's requirements", M1–M3). Rough marks
are enough: `diagnostics.js` finds the lift inside them. The frame source (s9) gives frames and thumbnails without
`<video>` seeking, and its frame times (`frameTimes`, contract 5 since s35); a one-frame step goes through s38's stepper,
since a step with `frameAt` takes 166–416 ms on the Fairphone (c34); the queue (s12) takes the job and gives the
estimate. Screens follow the mockups (docs/ui-design.md, design/mockups/); what they don't settle goes under "Needs a decision", and screenshots stay out of git: describe them.

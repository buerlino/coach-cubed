# d14: M1–M3, marking a lift (dirac, sonnet, branch `wp/dirac-mark`)
**Why:** nordmu's design: drag the video under a fixed playhead, Set start, Set end, then Analyse; the marks as tight as
possible, since processing time grows with their length (docs/ui-design.md, "nordmu's requirements", M1–M3). Rough marks
are enough: `diagnostics.js` finds the lift inside them. The frame source (s9) gives frames and thumbnails without
`<video>` seeking; the queue (s12) takes the job and gives the estimate. Screens follow the mockups (docs/ui-design.md, design/mockups/); what they don't settle goes under "Needs a decision", and screenshots stay out of git: describe them.
**Read:** docs/ui-design.md; design/mockups/Mark.dc.html; design/mockups/MarkEnd.dc.html; design/mockups/MarkReady.dc.html;
app/README.md; the folders `app/src/media/`, `app/src/analysis/` and `app/src/flow/`.
**Red first:** yes
1. Tests first, `tests/app/mark.test.js`, on the marking state as plain functions: the playhead's time from the drag and
   the filmstrip's scale, Set start and Set end (end after start, a few frames apart at least), one-frame steps at the
   clip's real rate, Redo, the marked length and its analysis time from s12's estimate, Analyse queuing a job with the
   range and handing back to the flow. They fail on phase-0.
2. The screen in its three states as drawn: the filmstrip from the frame source's thumbnails moving under the fixed
   playhead, the frame under it decoded off the input path so dragging never blocks, Set start, Set end, ±1 frame, the
   length and the time, Analyse, Redo.
3. What to measure on the Fairphone (how smooth the drag is, the time to show a frame) goes in the report, for c26.

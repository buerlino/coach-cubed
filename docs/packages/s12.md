# s12: the analysis job and its queue (schrödinger, opus, branch `wp/schroedinger-analysis-queue`)
**Why:** Analyse doesn't block: a marked lift joins a queue and the user carries on (decisions.md, "What 0.1.0 is"); Q1
shows progress by stage and the time left, and M1–M3 show what a marked range will cost (docs/ui-design.md). After s9,
s10 and s11 the pieces exist: the frame source, the pose engine, the scoring adapter; s32 keeps the lift's video as a
cut of the marked range (nordmu, D6). This joins them.
**Read:** docs/app-plan.md ("The contracts", "Risks"); docs/ui-design.md (M1–M3 and Q1; "What the mockups add": the
cost while marking); src/track/lift-window.mjs; tests/fixtures/README.md (the track format); app/README.md; the folders
`app/src/contracts/`, `app/src/media/`, `app/src/pose/`, `app/src/scoring/` and `app/src/store/`; s32's
app/src/media/cut.mjs.
**Red first:** yes
1. Tests first, `tests/app/analysis.test.js`, with a fake frame source, engine and store: a job goes queued, reading,
   pose, scoring, done, one at a time, in order; progress per stage with done and total; the time left from the measured
   ms per frame (a running median); cancel stops between frames and resets the engine; a failed job says why; the track
   and the result saved through the store; a job cut off (the WebView frozen in the background, the app closed) resumes
   or restarts when the app is back, never a silent hang; the estimate M1–M3 shows before Analyse (seconds per second of
   video from the last measured rate, with ui-design.md's 2.9 s, the Fairphone's, as the first-run default, labelled so);
   at Analyse the cut is stored (s32's `keepVideo`) before the job starts, and the job's source is that stored cut with
   its range in the cut's timeline; after a restart the job finds its source in the store. They fail on phase-0.
2. `app/src/analysis/`: at Analyse the queue first calls s32's `keepVideo`; the job's source is the stored cut, never a
   `File`, and its range is in the cut's timeline. The job (every frame of the range through the engine, the track in the
   fixture format with its `meta`, s11's adapter) and the queue (a plain module the UI subscribes to; it outlives screen
   changes; one engine).
3. A committed fixture track fed in place of the engine gives the same result as the adapter alone.
**Done when:**
- jobs pass contract 2's validator, and tracks match the fixture format;
- the tests show the cut stored before the job starts and the job's source found in the store after a restart;
- `npm test`, `npm --prefix app test` and the Python tests pass.

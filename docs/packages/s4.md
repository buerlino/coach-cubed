title, cue, unit and phase label, its tiles and reference line, its worst moment, and its overlay drawn on a canvas, and
today those are reachable only through the old page's mounted panel: `DIAGNOSTIC_SETS` is private, and
`Diagnostics.overlay(ctx, time)` reads the mounted state and `el.video()`. A1 and A2 (docs/ui-design.md) draw all of them.

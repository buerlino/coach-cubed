**Why:** nordmu, 2026-11-04 (docs/decisions.md, "What 0.1.0 is", the late entry): 0.1.0 is milestone A, importing a
clip and getting the cues, as a PWA on coach-cubed.org. The gym session (milestone B: R1, R2, R3, R3b, recording) is
built but ships **off by default** behind a "preview" switch; Noah finishes it after the handover (`c30b`, `c31`,
checkpoint B). Today it is always on. Its entry points on `phase-0` (checked with `git grep`):
- **H's gym card:** app/src/ui/screens/H.mjs draws `gymCard(...)` (app/src/ui/record-model.mjs, about line 116) as
  `[data-card="session"]`: "Gym session" goes to `setup` (R1); while a session runs it reads "Back to the session"
  with the clock and goes to the session's live state (`live` or `delayed`).
- **The flow:** app/src/flow/flow.mjs: `home` → `setup` is the only way into R1 (`TRANSITIONS.home`), and
  `home` → `live`/`delayed` only while `sessionRunning()` (createFlow's option, main.mjs gives

  asks for the camera. app/src/main.mjs makes the one session and the flow.
- **S1:** app/src/ui/screens/S1.mjs's first section "Gym session" draws `recordingView(settings)`
  (app/src/ui/settings-model.mjs): the replay mode, the replay's length or the delay, the buffer, the camera, the
  quality, **the default lift**, and the note "Up to 30 fps; less in dim light.". The default lift is milestone A's
  too: L1 rings it (`startLift()` in home-model.mjs, `liftTiles()` in lift-model.mjs, d41 and d53), so it must
  stay when the switch is off.
- **Welcome** (welcome-model.mjs's `WELCOME`) doesn't mention recording: nothing to change there.
- **The settings record:** app/src/store/settings.mjs (`DEFAULT_SETTINGS`, `FIELDS`, `readSettings()`, which reads an
  older record field by field, each missing or bad field taking its default).

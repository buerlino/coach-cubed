# c31: a session on the Fairphone, 30–60 minutes (curie, sonnet, branch `wp/curie-session-soak`)
**Why:** Checkpoint B is nordmu's real gym session with the app (docs/'s app-plan.md, milestone B). Before it, the
session runs the way he will use it, for long enough that heat, battery, storage and memory show: 30–60 minutes of
recording and analysing, in the APK and in Brave. Step 3.4's 35-minute heat run (no throttling, about 3.4 % of the
battery on USB power) ran the spike's bench, not the app (docs/ui-design.md, "Feasibility, from Phase 3"). `c30` and
`c30b` measured the camera and the session in short runs. This run is the long one. It writes no app code unless
something breaks, and a fix then goes under "Needs a decision" for its own package.
**Read:** spike/pose-bench/phone-sample.sh; spike/capture-check/drive.mjs; app/tools/check-apk.mjs;
pipeline/stay-awake.sh. Read these yourself, by section: the app's README (in app/, about 1,700 lines) "Capture and the
rolling buffer" with its "On the phone", "R1 and R2, the session", "R3, the replay", "R3b, the delayed view", "The
analysis", "The store" and "S1, settings and about"; the reports of c26, c30 and c30b (`git log --grep`), for the
baselines and how the phone was driven.
1. The phone (docs/pipeline.md, "The phone"): by day only, **with nordmu present**: it's his own phone, for up to two
   hours. Take its lock (`pipeline/devlock.sh acquire 4d42e62f curie-<run> 1800`, renewed every 20 minutes), keep it
   awake with `pipeline/stay-awake.sh 4d42e62f`, and name the phone in every adb command (`adb -s 4d42e62f …`). If the lock
   is busy or the phone is missing, say so in the report and stop. Start with the battery above 60 % and below 35 °C.
   Stop early if the battery falls under 10 % or its temperature passes 45 °C, and say which.
2. The session, through the app's own screens, driven over CDP: at least 30 minutes in the APK, then at least 30 in
   Brave, both with the default settings (the replay mode, a 60 s buffer, a 20 s replay). Every 2 minutes a lift is
   kept on R3, a 10–15 s range is marked on M, and it is analysed while recording goes on. **What the camera films**
   (nordmu, 2026-11-02): the laptop's screen, playing an example lift clip on loop, full screen. The clip is the
   lateral raise's `lateral-raise-cable-1`, read in place from the `phase-0` worktree's gitignored
   `tests/fixtures/local/example/` and never copied. So every kept lift has a person in it and the analysis does its
   full work. The phone stands still facing the screen, and nordmu starts the playback if the run can't. Note in the
   report whether the analyses found the lifter.
3. Sampled every 30 s: the battery's level and temperature and the thermal status (`dumpsys battery`,
   `dumpsys thermalservice`); the app's memory (phone-sample.sh); the capture's real rate and dropped frames; each job's
   ms per frame and its time from Analyse to done; the queue's length. At the start and the end: the lifts' bytes (S1's
   storage numbers) and the phone's free space. At the end, every kept lift opens in A1 with its cards.
4. The numbers go into the app's README, as a section "A session on the phone": a table every 5 minutes for each shell,
   the storage the session took, and anything that throttled, slowed down or failed.
5. Recordings and kept lifts stay on the phone for the run only. Never commit a recording, a frame or a screenshot.
**Done when:**
- at least 30 minutes ran in each shell, or the report says where and why it stopped;
- the report has step 3's table every 5 minutes for each shell, the storage the session took, and a verdict for
  checkpoint B: ready, or not ready with the reasons;
- every kept lift opened in A1 at the end, or the report lists those that didn't;
- the phone is put back: `pipeline/stay-awake.sh 4d42e62f --restore-real`, the forwards and reverses removed, the app
  uninstalled, Brave's site data for the page's origin cleared, the recordings deleted;
- `npm test` and `python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -p 'test_*.py'` pass.

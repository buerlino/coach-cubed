# s38: one-frame steps from a cache (schrödinger, opus, branch `wp/schroedinger-frame-steps`)
**Why:** M1–M3 mark a lift by stepping one frame at a time (docs/ui-design.md, "nordmu's requirements"), and on the
Fairphone a step with `frameAt` takes 166 ms with a keyframe every second and 416 ms, up to 828 ms, with a phone
camera's keyframes every 2–5 s (c34, app/README.md "The frame source", "On the phone"): every call decodes again from
the keyframe before it. nordmu decided on 2026-11-02, from c34's report, to add a stepping cache before d14 builds
marking: the frames around the playhead are decoded once and kept, so a step is a lookup in both directions.
**Read:** app/src/media/frame-source.mjs; app/src/media/decode.mjs; app/src/media/sample-table.mjs;
app/src/contracts/frame-source.mjs (contract 5, with s35's `frameTimes`); tests/app/frame-source.test.js;
tests/app/fake-webcodecs.js; app/README.md ("The frame source" is past its first 400 lines: read it by section);
app/debug/media.mjs and app/tools/check-media.mjs (c34's and s9's measurements of a step).
**Red first:** yes
1. Tests first, in tests/app/frame-source.test.js with the fake decoder, which counts what it decodes; they fail on
   phase-0:
   - a stepper opened at a time gives that frame, and +1 and −1 steps give the neighbouring frames by `frameTimes`,
     with no decode for a frame already held;
   - steps across a keyframe group, forwards and backwards, get their frames from a background refill started before
     the step needs them (a step that outruns the refill waits for it and never decodes the same group twice);
   - the cache holds at most its budget (frames and bytes, the caller's or a default the README states), evicts the
     frames farthest from the playhead and closes each frame it drops; `close()` closes every frame and stops the
     refill; the frame source's `close()` closes its steppers;
   - frames come out upright and at the size the caller asks for (display size, not the file's 1080p), as thumbnails do;
   - a decoder error or stall during a refill comes out as the frame source's MediaError on the next step, never a hang.
2. The stepper in the frame source (its name and shape in contract 5, since d14 builds on it: a package's own
   interface, agreed here before the UI uses it); `frameAt` and `frames` stay as they are.
3. app/debug/media.mjs's step measurement gets the stepper beside `frameAt`, so a later phone run (c26) compares
   both; in headless Brave (`node app/tools/check-media.mjs`, extended) the step time with and without the cache,
   in the report. The default budget's memory at display size, worked out from the frame size and stated.
4. app/README.md's "The frame source" describes the stepper and its budget.
**Done when:**
- a step to a held frame decodes nothing, and a step across a group boundary decodes that group once (the tests above);
- the cache never holds more than its budget and closes every frame it drops, and `close()` leaves no frame open;
- contract 5 names the stepper; `frameAt`, `frames` and `thumbnails` are unchanged;
- the report gives the headless Brave step times with and without the cache;
- `npm test`, `npm --prefix app test` and the Python tests pass.

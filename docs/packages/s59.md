# s59: s12's watchdog from c26's numbers: the stall time, restarts, tick and Q1's time left (schrödinger, sonnet, branch `wp/schroedinger-watchdog-values`)
**Why:** s12's watchdog values were provisional until the phone measured them (the app's README, "The analysis": "The
30 s is provisional … `s59` sets the values from these"). `c26` measured them on the Fairphone (report `e0a81e0`,
"Numbers", "s12 (queue.mjs)"): over 40 jobs the longest wait between two progress
events was 1,044 ms (the engine's warm-up for a job started right after an app start, 768–1,044 ms in the APK),
otherwise the first frame at 197–382 ms; the largest known wait is c20's cold engine create, 4.35 s. Progress comes
every 70–87 ms (p90), about 12 events a tick. No job hung or restarted. And Q1's first estimate was wrong: at "reading
0/1" (about 130 ms) it showed the last job's rate in seconds per second of video, unscaled for this clip's frame rate:
"~19 s left" for a job that took 9.8 s, "~7 s left" for 15.6 s; from "reading 1/1" on it was within 0.6 s. The cause
is app/src/analysis/job.mjs:85, `etaS: rate ? rate.sPerS * (t1 - t0) : null`: the cut isn't open yet, so the frame
count isn't known. From "reading 1/1" on, `etaFrom()` uses ms per frame times the range's frames, which needs no
scaling.
**nordmu, 2026-11-04** (docs/decisions.md, on c26's report, as recommended): `stallMs` 30 s → **20 s** (4.6× c20's
cold create, and a real hang is found 10 s sooner); `tickMs` stays **1,000 ms** (5 % of the stall time);
`maxRestarts` stays **1** (a restart costs an engine create plus the job again, up to 39 s in c26's runs); Q1 shows
"estimating…" until the cut is open.

# c30b: recording and analysis together, on the Fairphone (curie, opus, branch `wp/curie-session-phone`)
**Why:** A lift is processed on the phone while the user is still training (decisions.md, "What 0.1.0 is",
2026-10-31). The plan's c30 asks four things (docs/'s app-plan.md, milestone B): that the queue runs while recording
goes on, that the camera works in the WebView (`c30` did that), that the screen stays on, and what Android's freeze of a
backgrounded WebView does to a running job. What is known so far:
- s12's queue runs one job at a time and pauses while the page is hidden, and a frozen WebView runs nothing (the app's
  README, "The analysis");
- c27's capture holds a wake lock and goes `interrupted` when the camera track ends ("Capture and the rolling buffer");
- the spike recorded, decoded and posed together for 35 minutes without throttling, but that was the bench, not the
  app (docs/ui-design.md, "Feasibility, from Phase 3").
This run checks the app's own session on the phone.
**Read:** app/src/media/capture.mjs; app/src/analysis/queue.mjs; app/src/analysis/index.mjs; app/tools/check-apk.mjs;
spike/capture-check/drive.mjs; spike/pose-bench/phone-sample.sh; pipeline/stay-awake.sh. Read these yourself, by
section: the app's README (in app/, about 1,700 lines) "The analysis", "Capture and the rolling buffer" with c30's "On
the phone", "R1 and R2, the session", "R3, the replay", "R3b, the delayed view", "M1–M3, marking a lift", "Q1,
analysing" and "The Android shell"; c26's and c30's reports (`git log --grep`), for the baseline numbers and how the
phone was driven.
1. The phone (docs/pipeline.md, "The phone"): by day only, and nordmu must know the run is happening, since the
   Fairphone is his own phone. Take its lock (`pipeline/devlock.sh acquire 4d42e62f curie-<run> 1800`, renewed for a
   longer run), keep it awake with `pipeline/stay-awake.sh 4d42e62f` (except in step 3's wake-lock check), and name the
   phone in every adb command (`adb -s 4d42e62f …`). If the lock is busy or the phone is missing, say so in the report
   and stop.
2. In the APK and in Brave, run a real session through the app's own screens, driven over CDP; nordmu taps where a
   prompt needs it. Medians of at least five runs:
   - a lift kept on R3 and analysed while recording goes on: the job's ms per frame, against c26's without recording,
     and the capture's real rate and dropped frames while the job runs;
   - M while recording: the one-frame step's time (s38's stepper), against c26's and c34's;
   - R3b's delayed view while a job runs: the delay on screen against the setting, and the frames skipped.
3. The screen stays on: first `pipeline/stay-awake.sh 4d42e62f --restore-real`, and write the phone's real screen
   timeout in the report. Then R2 must stay on for twice that timeout, in each shell, through the app's wake lock alone.
4. The app in the background, during recording and during a job, in each shell:
   - the home key (`adb -s 4d42e62f shell input keyevent KEYCODE_HOME`) for 30 s and for 3 minutes, then back;
   - the screen off and on.
   For each, record what the camera track, the encoder, the ring and the job did, and what R2 and Q1 show on return.
   The session must come back usable or say why it can't. It must never come back silently stopped.
5. Fix what breaks inside the session's own code (app/src/media/'s capture, the session module, R2's interrupted
   state), each fix with a test that fails on phase-0. Anything larger goes under "Needs a decision", with a
   recommendation.
6. The numbers go into the app's README, in the "On the phone" part of "Capture and the rolling buffer" (and "The
   analysis" for the job beside recording), each with how it was measured.
**Done when:**
- every check of steps 2–4 ran in the APK and in Brave, or the report says where it stopped and why;
- the report has a table per step, each number with its method, and says plainly whether R2 stayed on through the
  wake lock alone;
- each fix of step 5 has a test that fails on phase-0's code (the report names them), or the report says there was
  none;
- the phone is put back: `pipeline/stay-awake.sh 4d42e62f --restore-real`, the forwards and reverses removed, the app
  uninstalled, the recordings deleted;
- `npm test`, `npm --prefix app test`, `npm --prefix app run test:page` and
  `python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -p 'test_*.py'` pass.

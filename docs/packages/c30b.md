# c30b: recording and analysis together, on the Fairphone (curie, opus, branch `wp/curie-session-phone`)
**Why:** A lift is processed on the phone while the user is still training (decisions.md, "What 0.1.0 is",
2026-10-31). The plan's `c30b` row asks three things (docs/'s app-plan.md, milestone B): that the queue runs while
recording goes on, that the screen stays on, and what Android's freeze of a backgrounded WebView does to the capture
and a running job (the camera in the WebView was `c30`'s). What is known so far:
- s12's queue runs one job at a time and pauses while the page is hidden, and a frozen WebView runs nothing (the app's
  README, "The analysis");
- c27's capture holds a wake lock and goes `interrupted` when the camera track ends ("Capture and the rolling buffer");
  since c61 (merged 2026-11-04, on the fake camera) a camera that fails at the resume leaves it `failed` with the ring
  held, and R2's and R3b's "Try again" resumes into the same ring, while Replay and Keep stay enabled;
- the spike recorded, decoded and posed together for 35 minutes without throttling, but that was the bench, not the
  app (docs/ui-design.md, "Feasibility, from Phase 3").
This run checks the app's own session on the phone.

1. The phone (docs/pipeline.md, "The phone"): by day only, and nordmu must know the run is happening, since the
   Fairphone is his own phone. Take its lock (`pipeline/devlock.sh acquire 4d42e62f curie-<run> 1800`: it waits up to
   30 minutes for the lock, which then has no expiry; take it once, never again while you hold it, and release it at
   the end with `pipeline/devlock.sh release 4d42e62f curie-<run>`), keep it awake with `pipeline/stay-awake.sh 4d42e62f` (except in step 3's wake-lock check), and name the
   phone in every adb command (`adb -s 4d42e62f …`). If the lock is busy or the phone is missing, say so in the report
   and stop.
2. In the APK and in Brave, run a real session through the app's own screens, driven over CDP; nordmu taps where a
   prompt needs it; the APK and Brave set up as c30's report says. Medians of at least five runs:
   - a lift kept on R3 and analysed while recording goes on: the job's ms per frame, against c26's without recording
     (median gaps: APK 80.5–82.7 ms, Brave 70.0–71.1 ms), and the capture's real rate and dropped frames while the job
     runs;
   - M while recording: the one-frame step's time (s38's stepper), against c26's (70–92 ms) and c34's. c26's first walk
     after a start stepped at ~320 ms (its "Needs a decision" 6): time M on two clips in either order, and say which
     run was the first after a start;
   - R3b's delayed view while a job runs, at 5, 10 and 20 s (`button[data-delay]`): the delay on screen (R3b's canvas
     `data-live-us` minus `data-frame-us`, as the page test's delayed walk reads it), the frames drawn per second
     (`data-frames`), the skips, the app's memory beside the encoder and the pose engine (phone-sample.sh; c26: the APK
     940–960 MB during an analysis, 1.1–1.4 GB with M open; Brave's figure counts all its tabs; the hardware encoder
     and decoder run in Android's codec service, which it doesn't count: say so), and the frames' rotation in
     portrait; a recommendation for `MAX_LAG_US` in delayed.mjs (0.3 s, d29b's guess) under "Needs a decision". The
     skips (`stats.skips` of delayed.mjs) aren't on the shipped screen: add `canvas.dataset.skips` from it in R3b.mjs,
     with a test in tests/app/delayed.test.js, or count them on a debug page. `data-live-us` comes from delayed.mjs's
     own estimate, so once, nordmu compares R3b with the live corner while the camera films a running clock;
   - R3 with nordmu: the countdown "Not this one: back to live in 0:25" (`COUNTDOWN_S` in lookback-model.mjs, 25 s,
     provisional; nordmu, 2026-11-03); his verdict in the report, a change under "Needs a decision".
3. The screen stays on: first `pipeline/stay-awake.sh 4d42e62f --restore-real`, and write the phone's real screen
   timeout in the report. Then R2, and R3b in a delayed session, must stay on for twice that timeout, in each shell,
   through the app's wake lock alone.
4. The app in the background, during recording and during a job, in each shell:
   - the home key (`adb -s 4d42e62f shell input keyevent KEYCODE_HOME`) for 30 s and for 3 minutes, then back;
   - the screen off and on;
   - another app takes the camera (c27's report): nordmu opens the phone's camera app by hand (docs/pipeline.md: never
     touch other apps) for 30 s, then back: R2 and R3b show "Camera stopped", and the resume
     (`button[data-record="resume"]`) records on into the same ring. If nordmu can keep the camera app holding the
     camera while coach is on screen (split screen), tap the resume then: c61's `camera-busy` panel with "Try again"
     (`button[data-record="retry"]`), Replay (R2) or "Keep this lift" (R3b) still enabled, and after the camera app is
     closed, "Try again" records on into the same ring.
   For each, record what the camera track, the encoder, the ring and the job did, and what R2 and Q1 show on return.
   The session must come back usable or say why it can't. It must never come back silently stopped.
5. Fix what breaks inside the session's own code (app/src/media/'s capture, app/src/session.mjs, and the "Camera
   stopped" and failed states of R2 and R3b in ui/screens/, ui/'s record-model.mjs (`resumeCapture`, `liveView`) and
   delayed-model.mjs (`delayedView`)), each fix with a test that fails on phase-0. Anything larger goes under "Needs a
   decision", with a recommendation. A camera lost between the ring's start and the wake lock's grant counts as a
   running session (R2 shows "Camera stopped", the ring is kept; nordmu, 2026-11-04, as c61 built it): not a bug.
6. The numbers go into the app's README, in the "On the phone" part of "Capture and the rolling buffer" (and "The
   analysis" for the job beside recording), each with how it was measured; they replace R3b's "What waits for the
   phone (`c30`)" and what c30 left there named `c30b`.

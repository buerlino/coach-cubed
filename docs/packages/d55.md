3. The cue, as on the round-1 page (its H): `components/more-below.mjs`, drawn once by the app's shell for every
   screen. While the page can scroll further down, a fade at the bottom edge of the window with a ⌄ button (44 px,
   `aria-label="More below"`) that scrolls down by most of a window; it goes at the bottom, with nothing to scroll, and
   while a sheet (A3) is open. It follows scrolling, resizing and content that grows (a ResizeObserver on the app),
   lets taps through the fade, keeps the safe-area inset, and scrolls without animation under
   `prefers-reduced-motion`. M fits its window in portrait (d54), so it shows there only in landscape.

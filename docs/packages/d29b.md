# d29b: R3b, the delayed view, as a setting (dirac, opus, branch `wp/dirac-delayed-view`)
**Why:** D2 makes R3b, the delayed view, a setting beside R3's replay. The screen shows the camera 5–20 s late, with
a small live view in the corner, and there is nothing to touch until "Keep this lift": no tapping with chalky hands,
like the video-delay mirrors gyms use (docs/ui-design.md, R3b and "What the mockups add"). The mockup is
design/mockups/DelayedView.dc.html: REC and the clock, "10 s behind live", LIVE in the corner, the delay (5, 10, 15
or 20 s), "Keep this lift, then mark start and end", End session. R1's replay mode (Replay button or Delayed view) is
in design/mockups/RecordSetup.dc.html. `d28` left its place on R1 free. The settings already hold `replayMode` and
`delayS` (5–20 s, app/src/store/settings.mjs), and S1 shows the delay disabled. `c27`'s `follow(t)` gives the ring's
chunks from a keyframe, then the live ones; s9's app/src/media/decode.mjs turns chunks into frames. This is the split
`d29` names: R3 first, then R3b.

1. Tests first, failing on phase-0 (plain modules, in Node, on fakes):
   - the flow has a state `delayed` with the screen ID `R3b` (module `screens/R3b.mjs`). `setup -> delayed`,
     `delayed -> mark`, `delayed -> home`, `delayed -> queued`, `mark -> delayed` and `queued -> delayed` are legal.
     A session's live state is `live` or `delayed`, by its `replayMode` when it started. Every way back to a running
     session goes to that state, never to `live` by name: M after Analyse, M's close, Q1's "Back to the session", and
     H's "Back to the session";
   - the delayed frames (a plain module in app/src/media/, e.g. `delayed.mjs`, on the fake decoder and a fake clock):
     it follows the capture from the newest frame's time minus the delay, and decodes with decode.mjs. Each frame is
     handed out once its capture time plus the delay has come, so the delay on screen stays within a frame interval of
     the setting. A decoder that falls behind skips to the next keyframe instead of drifting. A change of delay
     restarts it at the new delay. Every frame the screen doesn't take is closed, as is every frame still held at
     `close()`, and the decoder with them;
   - R3b's view: the clock, `10 s behind`, and the delays (5, 10, 15, 20 s, each inside `RANGES.delayS`), with the
     chosen one pressed; a choice saves `delayS`. "Keep this lift" goes to `mark` with
     `{ file, lift, from: 'delayed' }`, the file being the session's `snapshot(replayS + delayS)`: the replay's length
     before the frame on screen, and everything since;
   - R1's replay mode is Replay or Delayed view, saved as `replayMode`, and Start goes to `live` or `delayed` by it.
     S1's delay row is enabled.

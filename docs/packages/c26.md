# c26: milestone A on the Fairphone (curie, opus, branch `wp/curie-milestone-a-phone`)
**Why:** Milestone A is done when it works offline on the target phone. pose-spike.md's numbers (in `docs/`) came from
the bench, not the app, and s7 checked the from-source bundle on the CPU only. This run measures the app itself, as the
APK and as a PWA in Brave, gathers what d14 and d16 asked to have measured, and checks the from-source keypoints
against npm's on real clips (step 5.3's real-clip parity). s9's and s32's list (decoding, seeking, frame steps, the
cut's memory) and the engine's create and reset times were measured earlier, by c34 and c20 (nordmu, 2026-11-02):
repeat one here only where the app's own screens could change it.
**Read:** spike/pose-bench/README.md;
spike/pose-bench/phone-sample.sh; spike/pose-bench/summarize.mjs; spike/pose-bench/compare.mjs;
spike/pose-bench/difftrack.mjs; app/src/analysis/queue.mjs (the watchdog: `stallMs`, `tickMs`, `maxRestarts`);
the reports of s12, c20, c34, d14, d16, d53, d54, d55 and d58 (`git log --grep`). Read these yourself, by section (they
are long, so they aren't bundled whole): docs/'s app-plan.md (the "**Checkpoint A:**" paragraph near line 226, and
"## Risks"); the app's README (in app/, about 1,860 lines) "The analysis", "Q1, analysing", the two "On the phone"
parts (under "The frame source" and "Keeping the video"), "H, home, the picker and L1", "M1–M3, marking a lift", "The
Android shell" and "Offline: the service worker".
1. The phone (docs/pipeline.md, "The phone"): by day only. The Fairphone is nordmu's own phone and goes into airplane
   mode, so nordmu must know the run is happening. Take its lock (`pipeline/devlock.sh acquire 4d42e62f curie-<run>
   1800`: it waits up to 30 minutes for the lock, which then has no expiry; take it once, never again while you hold
   it, and release it at the end with `pipeline/devlock.sh release 4d42e62f curie-<run>`), keep it awake with
   `pipeline/stay-awake.sh 4d42e62f`, and name it in every adb command (`adb -s 4d42e62f …`). If the lock is busy or
   the phone is missing, say so in the report and stop.
2. The APK (c20's script) and the PWA in Brave: the PWA served from the laptop through `adb -s 4d42e62f reverse` and
   installed; then remove the forwards (`adb -s 4d42e62f reverse --remove-all`), stop the laptop's server and switch on
   airplane mode. Only then do the offline runs start, so a cache miss shows. The four example clips, read from the
   `phase-0` worktree's `tests/fixtures/local/` (never copied into the repo; pushed to the phone for the run and deleted
   after), each picked through H's "Import a video" (`d13`), its lift chosen on L1 (`d53`), its range marked on M with
   the step buttons (`[data-mark]`, `d54`) and analysed. Once per shell, a real tap on H's "Import a video" (CDP
   `Input.dispatchTouchEvent`) must open Android's picker (d53's report: the headless tests set the input directly);
   How the clips get in (nordmu, 2026-11-03): in the APK as c34's tool does it (check-media-apk.mjs copies them into
   the debug app's own storage with `run-as`; `DOM.setFileInputFiles` works there). In Brave, which isn't debuggable
   and has no laptop server offline, push the four clips to the phone's Downloads (`adb -s 4d42e62f push … /sdcard/Download/`)
   before airplane mode, and pick each one through Android's picker yourself: `adb -s 4d42e62f shell input tap` at
   the place a screenshot (`adb -s 4d42e62f exec-out screencap -p`, read it yourself) shows, one tap at a time,
   checking the screen after each. nordmu isn't needed for this; if the picker can't be driven, say where it stopped.
   Delete the clips from Downloads after the run.
3. Per clip and shell: the total time, ms per frame, memory (phone-sample.sh), console errors (CDP); each stored track
   read back over CDP and scored against the ViTPose-L references with compare.mjs, as the bench's were; the frame,
   drag and step times the earlier reports asked for, the step with s38's cache beside `frameAt`'s (c34's
   app/debug/media.mjs and app/tools/check-media-apk.mjs measure both). Also the decoded frames the hardware decoder
   keeps open while the stepper sits on a frame: s38f1 bounded it at 2 in headless Brave, counted only on
   app/tools/check-media.mjs's page (s38f1's report, "Not done"); bring the count into app/debug/media.mjs for the
   phone. Medians of at least five runs where time allows.
   In Brave, portrait, on M with an example clip (all three are 1080×1920): `document.documentElement.scrollHeight`
   against `innerHeight` in M1, M2 and M3, and the boxes of the `[data-mark]` buttons and `.mark-text` before and after
   1, 2 and ↶ (`[data-mark="undo"]`), equal within 0.5 px (d54's report). With the four lifts kept: does H's ⌄ "More
   below" cue (`d55`) show while the list goes on below, and go at its end? The report says so, for nordmu.
   Also in Brave, portrait, after an analysis (d58's report): `scrollHeight` against `innerHeight` on A1 and on A2 (the
   page must not scroll; A1's cue list scrolls inside); A1's list fade (`.list-fade`) shows while the list goes on
   below and is gone at its end, after slow scrolls and after fast flicks (`adb -s 4d42e62f shell input swipe` with a
   duration of 50–100 ms, several in a row, then one back up): no fade left behind at the end, none missing above it,
   and a tap on a card under the fade still opens A2. A screenshot of each case, read yourself; the report says so,
   for nordmu. Landscape stays as built (the page scrolls; nordmu, 2026-11-03): don't measure it.
   c34's 1080p clips are in `~/.cache/coach/c34-clips` (app/tools/make-phone-clips.sh remakes them). For s12's
   provisional values (nordmu, 2026-11-02: set from this run, in a schrödinger package after it), per job: the longest
   wait between two progress events and what it was (the engine's start, cold and warm; the first frame; the reset,
   a message since s35), each stage's time (reading, pose, scoring), and the time left Q1 showed at each stage beside
   the real time to done.
4. Keypoint parity on real clips: pose-bench (npm's bundle) on the same clips in the same Brave, served through the
   forward before the offline runs or after them, never between; its tracks compared with the app's through
   difftrack.mjs (`--ref`); the mean and max px per keypoint. Serve pose-bench on a different port from the installed
   PWA's, so the PWA's service worker can't answer its requests.
5. The numbers into pose-spike.md, a section of their own, each with how it was measured; the parity numbers beside the
   times.
6. The installed APK allows no backup (c43): `adb -s 4d42e62f shell dumpsys package org.coach.placeholder` (the placeholder ID until c33) shows no
   `ALLOW_BACKUP` among its flags; the line goes in the report.

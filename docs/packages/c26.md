# c26: milestone A on the Fairphone (curie, sonnet, branch `wp/curie-milestone-a-phone`)
**Why:** Milestone A is done when it works offline on the target phone. pose-spike.md's numbers (in `docs/`) came from
the bench, not the app, and s7 checked the from-source bundle on the CPU only. This run measures the app itself, as the
APK and as a PWA in Brave, and gathers what s9, d14 and d16 asked to have measured.
**Read:** docs/app-plan.md ("Checkpoint A", "Risks"); app/README.md; spike/pose-bench/README.md;
spike/pose-bench/phone-sample.sh; spike/pose-bench/summarize.mjs; spike/pose-bench/compare.mjs; the reports of s9, d14
and d16 (`git log --grep`).
1. The phone (docs/pipeline.md, "The phone"): its lock, `pipeline/stay-awake.sh`, `adb -s 4d42e62f` only.
2. The APK (c20's script) and the PWA in Brave (served from the laptop through `adb reverse`, installed, then airplane
   mode): the four example clips, read from the `phase-0` worktree's `tests/fixtures/local/` (never copied into the
   repo; pushed to the phone for the run and deleted after), each imported, marked to its lift and analysed.
3. Per clip and shell: the total time, ms per frame, memory (phone-sample.sh), console errors (CDP); each stored track
   read back over CDP and scored against the ViTPose-L references with compare.mjs, as the bench's were; the frame,
   drag and step times the earlier reports asked for. Medians of at least five runs where time allows.
4. The numbers into pose-spike.md, a section of their own, each with how it was measured.
**Done when:**
- every clip ran offline in both shells, or the report says where it stopped and why;
- the verdicts agree with the bench's for the same clips, or the differences are listed;
- the phone is put back (`stay-awake.sh --restore-real`, forwards removed, the app uninstalled, the clips deleted);
- `npm test` and the Python tests pass.
